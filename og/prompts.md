## Note covers (ChatGPT, one chat, 17 Oct 2026)

One cover per article in /notes/, used on the card and at the top of the article. Same style as the series above, but the subject sits in the centre: nothing is overlaid on these.

Shared ending for every prompt: "Very dark navy background #050B24 with a faint blueprint grid. Thin glowing lines in sky blue #00A8F0 and ice blue #90D8F0, one warm accent in sun yellow #FFC81E. Subject centred, generous dark margins on all sides. Minimal, elegant, high contrast, precise technical drawing. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no apple logo, no people, no hands."

### note-apple-on-device-model
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article about the language model Apple built into every new iPhone. A large iPhone drawn as a glowing outline. Inside it, a small dense lattice of connected nodes glows like a compact brain, held entirely within the phone's outline. Far in the upper right a faint cloud outline is connected to the phone by a dashed line that fades out halfway: unused. The lattice's core node glows sun yellow.

### note-mcp-without-the-keys
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article about letting AI assistants into a product without handing them the keys. A diamond-shaped vault drawn in isometric outline, like a building plan. On its front, one narrow lit doorway. Outside, a small glowing orb (the assistant) receives a few rounded cards through that doorway along a single sun yellow line. Inside the vault, behind a second inner wall, a ring of keys hangs out of reach.

### note-ai-interview-dropped-facts
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article about an AI interview that lost facts clients gave it, and how they came back. On the left, a stream of small rounded speech-bubble shapes without any text flows right. In the middle, a fine grid like a sieve catches nineteen small round dots: eighteen glow sun yellow and settle into neat rows, one stays dim. Below the sieve, faint dashed trails show where dots used to fall through.

### note-the-translation-tax
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article about how much work goes into understanding what a client meant. On the left, a loose cloud of rough overlapping shapes, half drawn. On the right, a clean grid of rounded blocks. A single sun yellow thread connects them, but in the middle it winds into a long, tight knot that takes half the width before it straightens out.

### note-before-you-automate
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article saying that writing a process down doesn't mean it has left your head. In the centre, a page drawn in outline holds a neat column of rounded step blocks. From two of the steps, thin sun yellow threads still lead out of the page and back into a small labyrinth of nested diamonds behind it, where the missing knowledge still lives.

### note-watch-it-back
Wide landscape 16:9 cover for an article about recording a pitch or an AI agent, watching it back and rebuilding. A large closed loop, like a race track, drawn in perspective. Along it, a strip of empty rounded film frames; at one point a rounded play-triangle marker glows sun yellow. Three small gates sit on the loop at equal distances, the loop passing through each.
