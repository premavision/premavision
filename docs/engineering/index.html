Questions

What people ask before they start

Is this like having a technical co-founder?

It covers the work: technical decisions, vendor and hiring checks, a roadmap, and someone accountable for the architecture. Without equity or a title. We sell it as a fixed scope three months at a time, so you always know what you're paying for and can stop.

Which stacks?

TypeScript and Node, Python, Swift, Postgres, Docker, AWS and Cloudflare, plus AI stacks: LangGraph, LangSmith, OpenRouter, MCP. If yours is something else, the audit says honestly whether we are the right people.

What does an AI due diligence check that a normal one misses?

Whether the AI is a real system or a demo: what runs on which model, what it costs per user, how it is evaluated, what happens when the model provider changes, and whether the results in the deck can be reproduced on fresh cases.

Can you work with our existing team?

Yes, and usually that is the point: your team keeps building, and we make the calls that are expensive to get wrong, review the work, and set up what lets them ship safely.
