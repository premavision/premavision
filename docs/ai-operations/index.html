Questions

What people ask before they start

What happens to the people who do this work today?

They stop copying, sorting and chasing, and spend that time on the decisions and the clients. The judgement stays with them, and the map shows exactly which steps change for whom.

Why not just buy Zapier or an AI tool?

Often you should, and the map will say so. Tools automate one step; the trouble usually sits in the exceptions between steps and the decisions nobody wrote down. That part needs design first.

Which tools can you connect?

Anything with an API or webhooks: CRMs, helpdesks, email and calendars, Slack and Teams, databases, PagerDuty, Stripe, and AI assistants through MCP. If a tool has no API, we tell you what that costs.

We already run an AI agent. Can you audit it?

Yes. The audit takes a week, works from your traces and code, and ends with a ranked list of fixes, each with a price. If you have no traces yet, setting them up is the first fix.

What if the AI makes a mistake?

It will, sometimes. So every run is recorded with its reason, anything irreversible waits for a person, and each mistake someone reports becomes a test that runs before the next change.
