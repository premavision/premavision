What's an App Clip?

Missed calls · for contractors and service businesses

The calls you miss are the jobs you lose.

You are on a roof, under a house, or driving. The phone rings. By the time you call back, they have booked someone else. Nobody needs to explain that arithmetic to you.

I build the software that answers, takes the details, texts them back, and puts the job in front of you, so a missed call becomes a message instead of a lost job.

What it does

  • Answers when you cannot. Name, address, what is wrong, how urgent.
  • Texts the caller back within seconds, so they stop dialling the next name on the list.
  • Puts it where you already work: your calendar, your phone, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan, Square, whatever you use.
  • Follows up with the ones who never called back, on a schedule you set.
  • Asks for the review after the job is done, once, without nagging.

What I will tell you before you buy it

AI phone answering is not magic, and I would rather you heard the failure modes from me than found them yourself.

  • Addresses are the hard part. Street names get misheard. I test yours specifically, against your real service area, before it goes live.
  • Some callers want a human, immediately. So it says it is an assistant in the first sentence, and anyone who asks gets put through or gets you texted at once.
  • Florida requires everyone on a call to consent to recording. That is not a formality here, it is a felony statute. The announcement plays before recording starts, and anyone who declines gets a normal voicemail.
  • Texting has rules. Your business gets registered properly with the carriers, in your name, and STOP works instantly and permanently.
  • I will not do outbound robocalls or cold texting. That is where the lawsuits live, and I am not putting your company in that position.
