Why it needs a person

AI doesn't stay the way you shipped it

Models change under you

Providers update and retire models. The same prompt can answer differently next month, and nothing in your code changed.

AI fails plausibly

It rarely crashes. It gives a confident, slightly wrong answer, and the next step treats it as true.

Costs creep

A longer prompt here, a retry loop there. The bill grows faster than usage, and nobody notices until it arrives.

Users find new edges

Real people ask what the test set never imagined. Those cases are the most valuable data you have, if someone reads them.

Questions

What people ask before they sign

Can we start before launch?

Yes, and it is the best moment. Monitoring set up with the first real users catches the failures nobody tested for, while they are still cheap to fix.

Do we need to have built the system with you?

No. We start every retainer with a one-week audit of what you run today, priced at $2,400 and credited against the first month if you continue. If there are no traces to read yet, setting them up is the first fix.

How long do we commit for?

Three months at a time. After that it runs month to month and stops with 30 days notice.

What happens to unused hours?

They carry over one month, then expire. Work beyond the plan is quoted at a fixed price before we start it.

Who pays for the AI models and hosting?

You do, directly or at cost. The retainer is our work, never a markup on tokens.

Which tools do you watch?

LangSmith, Langfuse, OpenTelemetry traces or your own logs; OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Apple and open models; agents in LangGraph or your own code.
