Mobile apps with AI under the hood

Apple now ships a language model inside every recent iPhone. It runs on the device, costs nothing per request and keeps data on the phone. Siri, Shortcuts, Spotlight, widgets and the Action button all read one set of App Intents. Most apps use none of it.

  • · AI features on the device and on Apple's Private Cloud Compute
  • · Siri, Shortcuts and the Action button for your most repeated actions
  • · Apple Watch apps that move the work on from the wrist
  • · MCP connectors, so Claude and ChatGPT can use your product
  • · New apps, from zero to the App Store

Built first in Routine Run: 60+ App Intents, Apple's models write a routine from one sentence, and a PagerDuty page starts a runbook on the Watch.

AI interviews and expert systems

Your best expert can't sit in every conversation, and won't be with you forever. An AI interviewer asks what they would ask, one question at a time, and records the answers as structured data. Your experts decide what it knows and change it themselves.

  • · Client intake that ends in structured requirements, not a transcript
  • · Capturing a senior expert's judgement before they leave
  • · A back office to edit, test, publish and roll back, with no developer
  • · Quality measured on test runs, not guessed from complaints
  • · A person approves anything that can't be undone

Already running in Structed, which talks to your client and brings back a detailed brief. We can set it up for your domain instead of building from scratch: facts clients gave stopped going missing (18 of 19 recovered), and replies went from 20–60 seconds to 1–13.
